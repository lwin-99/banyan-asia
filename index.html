01 About Banyan

A quietly confident partner for the long road ahead.

Founded in 2022, Banyan is a full-service accounting and financial management firm based in Bangkok — designed for businesses that want to move decisively without getting caught in the complexity of Thai regulation.

Our Journey

Built for local and international clients.

We have grown from an accounting practice into a partner that handles company setup, licensing, and financial management end to end — delivering tailor-made solutions for businesses of every scale.

Our Vision

The trusted advisor — not the outsourced vendor.

To set a benchmark of excellence in the accounting and finance sector in Thailand, built on integrity, dependability, and exceptional service over the long term.

Our Mission

Accurate. Reliable. Timely.

To deliver financial information our clients can act on with confidence — holding ourselves to the highest ethical standards and cultivating relationships that contribute to their lasting success.

03 What We Offer

Deliverables your board
can rely on.

Every engagement is anchored to a clear set of outputs — produced on schedule, reviewed at partner level, and formatted so you can act on them immediately.

  • 01

    Bookkeeping

    Comprehensive bookkeeping that keeps your business running smoothly with reliable financial data and full transparency for stakeholders.

  • 02

    VAT & Withholding Filings

    We review and verify every input and output invoice, advise on VAT claims, and handle monthly submissions — compliant, efficient, and low-burden for your internal team.

  • 03

    Annual Financial Reporting & Tax Filing

    Accurate annual reporting and tax filing to keep you compliant with Thai regulations while optimising your overall financial outcome.

  • 04

    Half-Year Tax Estimation (PND 51)

    Preparation of the PND 51, verification of tax calculations, review of financial statements, and seamless submission to the Revenue Department — with no surprises.

  • 05

    Year-End Reporting & Preparation

    Close the fiscal year with confidence. We help local and international businesses meet regulatory requirements and streamline year-end processes from trial balance to filing.
